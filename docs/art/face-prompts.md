# Film it! — actor face prompts

Four grids, one prompt each. Each grid is **6×6 = 36 faces: the top 3 rows are women (18), the bottom 3 rows
are men (18)**. Generate each at **1024×1024** (that's plenty: each face becomes a 128 px portrait). Send me
the four images; I cut them into portraits and assign them to actors by gender, age and look.

**Rules for every grid (already inside each prompt):** same framing for all 36 (head and shoulders, centred,
same size), a plain cream background in every cell, no borders, no gaps, no text or labels, nothing crossing
cell edges.

Columns set the expression, matching the actor's strongest skill:

| Column | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Expression | warm smile (romance) | big grin (comedy) | serious, thoughtful (drama) | determined (action) | sly smirk, narrowed eyes (menace) | calm, neutral |

---

```
A 6x6 grid of 36 character portraits for a video game, low-poly 3D style with soft faceted shading, warm vintage
cinema palette (cream, brown, maroon, gold), friendly stylised proportions (slightly large heads), consistent
lighting from the top left. Every cell: one person, head and shoulders, centred, same size, plain cream
background (#F1E3CC), no borders, no gaps between cells, no text, nothing crossing cell edges.
Top three rows: young women aged about 20-30. Bottom three rows: young men aged about 20-30.
Columns, left to right, set the expression: 1 warm smile, 2 big toothy grin, 3 serious and thoughtful,
4 determined, 5 sly smirk with narrowed eyes, 6 calm neutral.
Vary skin tones widely in every row (very light to very dark), and vary hair: long, short, curly, braids,
buzz cut, bun, afro, ponytail, bob, wavy; hair colours black, brown, auburn, blonde, red, dyed pink or blue.
Some wear simple tops (t-shirt, hoodie, denim jacket, blouse), a few glasses, one cap per row at most.
```
